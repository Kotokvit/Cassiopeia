1.2 Cassiopeian Medical Terminology 
The following terms are officially adopted by the XRD Medical Division for clinical documentation: 
Gravitational Ligament Atrophy (GLA) 
Progressive degradation of uterine and pelvic ligaments caused by chronic 1.4g stress. Onset typically at 18-20 Cass years. Measured on GLA Scale 1-4. 
Cassiopeian Factor (CF) 
Composite hormonal index measuring: CF = (Testosterone x 0.4) + (Cortisol x 0.35) + (Estrogen x 0.25). Normal range: 1.8-2.4. CF > 3.0 indicates resonance overload. 
Technetium-Cortical Index (TCI) 
Ratio of Tc concentration in ovarian cortex to blood serum. Normal: 12-18. Below 10 indicates reproductive system degradation. 
Uterine Burnout Syndrome (UBS) 
Terminal state of uterine tissue after cumulative gravitational stress. Characterized by fibrosis, reduced vascularity, and loss of contractile capacity.

Maximum fetal mass supportable by placenta under 1.4g. Calculated as: PGT = Placental mass x 1.4 x k_tc where k_tc = 1 + [Tc] x 50. 
Resonance Pregnancy Premium (RPP) 
Extended fertility window available to active Resonators (Level B and above) due to enhanced Tc uptake. Adds 3-5 Cass years to reproductive window.

Sexual maturation in Cassiopeian females is governed by the interaction of gravitational stress and technetium-accelerated mitosis. The onset of reproductive capacity at 13 Cass years (18.5 Earth years) represents a precisely calibrated biological threshold.

On Cassiopeia, this threshold is elevated to 53.2 kg due to: 1. Increased skeletal mass requirement (Wolff Law adaptation) 2. Enhanced muscle fiber density for gravitational support 3. Reduced energy availability for growth (BMR elevated by 6%)

However, when accounting for ultra-dense tissue (rho = 1.47 g/cm3), the actual target mass becomes 53.2 kg, requiring additional 2.5 Cass years of growth. 
Why Not Earlier? (The 10.5 Year Question) 
A 10.5 Cass year old female (14.9 Earth years) has not completed the following critical developmental milestones: 
- Pelvic fusion: Iliac crest epiphysis not closed until 12.5-13 Cass years 
- Cardiovascular maturation: Left ventricular hypertrophy reaches adult proportions at 12+ Cass years 
- Hormonal axis stabilization: HPO axis reaches homeostasis at 12.8 Cass years (mean) 
- Technetium integration: Ovarian Tc uptake stabilizes at 13 Cass years 
Clinical determination: Prior to 13 Cass years, pregnancy would impose fatal cardiovascular stress due to incomplete maternal cardiac adaptation. Maternal mortality at ages 10-12 exceeds 34% even with optimal medical intervention.
